   stance: on a plain comparison, default and pull (stating the choice),
   or nudge?

   sugarcane argued via paddy methane - not what an sLUC deforestation EF
   measures) and unverifiable verdicts. One more (038) refused the
   two_period total outright. Single-value sLUC lookups pass (032/037
   answered correctly); it is specifically *comparative* EF phrasing that
