## For the agent team (file upstream)

1. **A refusal-to-pull cluster is the dominant failure: 4 of 8 fails**
   (`dataset_id_match` + `data_pull_exists` + `answered_without_data`
   together). The agent picks the AOI, then either denies the dataset
   exists or answers from the web instead of pulling:
   - ch-trend-028 read "counting all tree cover" as a **canopy threshold of
     0%** and refused ("the minimum threshold available is 10%") — it
     conflated the forest-type opt-out phrasing with the canopy parameter.
   - ch-trend-035 claims no dataset tracks crop production tonnage; sLUC
     (dataset 9) carries production volume.
   - ch-trend-051 claims no historical time series exists for disturbance
     alerts over 2024; integrated alerts is a daily series - and the
     refusal text still name-drops DIST-ALERT, which has left the catalog.
   - ch-trend-029 (soy emission-factor trend) skipped `pick_dataset`
     entirely and answered from wri.org / landcarbonlab.org blog posts with
     citations - the full 1-030 pattern (`answered_without_data` +
     `web_fallback` in one row).
2. **Primary-forest substitution, caught as designed**: ch-trend-067
   ("Counting all tree cover types in Indonesia...") silently pulled the
   `primary_forest` slice of the fires dataset and presented it as the
   answer. The figures are internally consistent (the info-only
   traceability check matched the chart to 0.00%), but they are the wrong
   slice - the exact slice-1 finding, now a scored `context_layer_match`
   fail on `no_selection` rows.
3. **Blog citations pad otherwise-correct data answers**: ch-trend-002 and
   ch-trend-017 pulled the data, answered from it correctly, then appended
   wri.org article links, failing `web_fallback` (the deliberate 1-030 /
   mt-007 signal: external citations in a data answer). Decide the product
   stance - if blog enrichment on numeric answers is wanted behaviour, the
   guard's spec needs revisiting; today it reads as provenance blurring.
4. **Chart hygiene**: ch-trend-043's bar chart mis-joins record sets (1/22
   records with null `loss_ha`) even though the headline answer is
   numerically exact. Known GOLD class, first sighting in CHALLENGE.

Worth stating explicitly: 63/71 rows cleanly picked the right dataset,
window and slice, pulled real analytics, and charted them - and on both
rows where the deterministic traceability check could compare the chart to
an expected figure, the delta was 0.00%. Retrieval-first scoring is
producing signal, and the eventual ground-truth work (Phase 2 W1/W2) has
something to bind to.
