## For the agent team (file upstream)

1. **Wrong-area substitution presented as success is the dominant failure
   mode: 26 of the 40 failing rows.** The agent never declines and never
   says "I could not find X"; it selects the closest string match and
   confidently reports success. Worst examples: "French Alps" selected
   **French Guiana** while the answer claims "focused on the French Alps"
   (ch-aoi-111); "Sumatra and Borneo" selected **Borno, Nigeria** for Borneo
   (ch-aoi-091); Cerrado → Cercado, Bolivia (118); Congo Basin → M'Banza
   Congo, Angola (119); the coordinates 51.5, -0.13 → a New Zealand covenant
   named "5-13-051" (129); Wakanda → Wakiso, Uganda, "which is likely the
   area you're looking for" (130). This is the fabrication class from the
   2026-08-28 outsider dry-runs, now measured: aoi-concepts (56%), aoi-vague
   (57%) and aoi-global (67%) fail almost exclusively through it. Per-row
   trace URLs are in the ledger.
2. **The nudge/select policy is inverted on both sides.** It nudges on names
   with one dominant referent (California, Sabah, USA, Köln, Pará, Paris,
   Victoria, California+Oregon: 8 `behaviour: select` rows) yet silently
   selects on genuinely ambiguous names (Santiago → Santiago de Cuba,
   Córdoba → Córdoba, Colombia, San José → an ejido landmark in Mexico,
   Georgia → the country: 4 `behaviour: nudge` rows). aoi-ambiguity is the
   weakest cohort at 40%.
3. **Candidate retrieval misses the dominant referent entirely on 3 of those
   nudges**: "USA" offered only Usa (Japan) and Usak (Turkey); "Köln" offered
   Italian and French Colognes but no German one; "Vai para o estado do Pará"
   offered Paraguay and Para, Suriname but not Brazil's Pará. These are the
   known acronym-passthrough and cross-language search gaps (SPEC-PR6
   lineage), independent of the nudge policy in item 2.
4. **Expansion resolves one admin level too deep.** "The four countries of
   the UK" selected 183 GADM level-2 districts instead of GBR.1-4
   (ch-aoi-094); "all counties in Kenya" selected 300 constituencies instead
   of the 47 counties (ch-aoi-097). Related: "Compare Pará and Paraná"
   selected BRA.16 **twice** (ch-aoi-090), a diacritic-blind match with no
   dedup of the selection.
5. **aoi-data-quality (50% vs target 80%, the largest gap) is entirely
   name-resolution**: England → the level-2 row literally named "NA"
   (GBR.1.100, the GADM NA-name bug); Zuid-Holland and South Holland → the
   Lincolnshire district; County Cork → Cork City; Holland → Noord-Holland.
