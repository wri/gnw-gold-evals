### Early finding, acted on mid-campaign: E1 claim extraction

The single-trial twin showed `answer_traceability` failing 9 rows: 1-027
is the designed-for true positive (679.28 vs 2,123.93 — the prose figure
absent from its own chart, exactly the parent-plan case), but ~5 of the
rest were the extractor latching onto **unitless bold counts and ranks**
("**2** datasets", "top **5**"). Actions, per PLAN §4's probation rule:

1. `first_bold_claim` now requires a unit/scale/percent on the number;
2. `answer_traceability` demoted to INFO_ONLY (dated comment in
   `buckets.py`) — re-admission requires a future 3-trial run with zero
   extraction false positives. The in-flight 3-trial run still scores the
   un-tuned E1; its E1 column is read as diagnostic only.

`scope_match`'s 13 failures, by contrast, correlate with real no-pull
behaviour (7 co-fail `data_pull_exists`, including 1-030) — it stays
scored pending the std table.
