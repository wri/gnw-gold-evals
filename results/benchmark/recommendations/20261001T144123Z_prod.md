## Headline

- **The original five types hold up (87–94%); the new jobs do not.** Monitoring (62%) and causal (43%) carry most of the gap, and they are mostly new questions. That is why new questions pass at 57% against 88% for existing ones; it is not mainly the drafted expectations (see "Case set").
- **Causal questions skip the data.** On 11 of the 42 failures the agent called `search_blogs` and answered from WRI articles instead of the driver or fire data, for example "Why did Brazil lose so much primary forest in 2016?" and "Did cocoa farming cause deforestation in Côte d'Ivoire?" All 3 attempts did this on most of those rows. This is the soybean emission-factor pattern from 29 September, now across a whole job.
- **Asking back is still the largest single behaviour:** a dataset-choice or place-choice nudge on 12 failures (for example "Is soy or cattle driving tree cover loss in Mato Grosso since 2015?", "How much alert area was flagged in Sabah last month?" which asked Malaysia or Yemen).
- **Wrong protected area on monitoring questions:** Sankuru resolved to Samburu (Kenya), the Okapi reserve to Ajai (Uganda), Gunung Leuser to WDPA 3030, Lopé and Yasuní to other areas on some attempts. The expected ids match prod `/api/aois`; these are picker misses, the PZB-1392 family.
- **Refusal rows: it guesses a place.** "Show me the Sundarbans", "the Cerrado", "Sumatra and Borneo", "the Congo Basin" and "Wakanda" (it chose Rwanda) each got a silent pick rather than a clarifying question.
- **Protected-area analytics are mostly working again:** 10 of 18 WDPA analytic questions passed. Virunga's alert pull still failed on 2 of 3 attempts ("no data"), so PZB-1406 should be checked before closing.
- **Rolling windows work:** no monitoring question failed its date check with the run-relative windows and a few days' tolerance.

## Recommendations

**Upstream (project-zeno):**

1. **Route "why" questions to the driver and fire data.** Causal prompts trigger `search_blogs` first; the agent should pull tree cover loss by driver (8) or fires (10) and explain from that, using articles only as context.
2. **Over-nudging**, again the largest lever: 12 failures. It nudges between datasets when the question names the job (drivers, alerts) and asks place disambiguation for clear cases (Sabah).
3. **WDPA name resolution** for parks and reserves on monitoring prompts (6 rows). Feed these into PZB-1392.
4. **Clarify instead of guessing** for biomes, islands and fictional places (6 refusal rows).

**Case set (before the November freeze):**

5. **Review the 62 drafted expectations.** Most failures on them are agent behaviour (above), but each needs a human pass, especially the judged `text` checks on causal rows.
6. `ch-causal-012` errored; re-run it.
7. Unblock `ch-mon-028` (saved-area triage) with a seeded test account, or drop it from the version.

**Harness:** none blocking. The relative-window tokens and tolerance behaved as intended.
