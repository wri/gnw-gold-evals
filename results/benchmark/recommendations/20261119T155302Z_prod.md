# BENCHMARK 2026-languages: full run in four languages, after the production update, 19 November 2026

**Full run of the languages companion `2026-languages` (draft), after the
production update of 19 November.** It is the "after" measurement for Spanish
and Indonesian and the first full measurement for Portuguese and French. The
language rates sit beside the English North Star and never count in it.

Production, default profile, 3 trials, 5 workers:

| Run | What it measured |
|---|---|
| `20261119T155302Z_prod` | All 502 companion members: 320 language questions (80 per language) and the 182 English twins that the companion holds |
| `20261119T115511Z_prod` | The canonical November run of `2026`, four hours earlier on the same day; the English side of the 133 pairs whose twin is a `2026` member |

The page `pages/20261119T155302Z_prod.json` combines them
(`benchmark_page.py --base`). All 320 pairs are measured; none is unpaired or
unscored. 2 of the 1,506 attempts got no reply (`ReadTimeout`, both French:
`ch-comp-153-fr`, `ch-mon-038-fr`); no question errored.

## How to read it

- No language scores below its English twins. Every interval includes zero.
  The Spanish gap of the before run (8 pairs against 2) is gone (4 against 4).
- With 80 pairs per language, a gap must be about 8 points before its
  interval excludes zero. The Indonesian and French leads are inside that.
- 46 of the 320 pairs differ between the two sides, 26 of them by two or
  three attempts. Nobody has read them for their cause (agent, judge or case)
  yet.
- The real user prompts and their twins are new cases. Some of their failures
  can still be case problems, on both sides of a pair alike.

## Caveats

- 133 twins were measured four hours before their language questions, on the
  same day and the same production agent (`max_days_apart` 0).
- The number reader can still match an expected figure by a fragment of a
  number grouped with spaces ("1 000 ha"). It can only turn a wrong answer
  into a right one.
- No native speaker has reviewed the translations or the English twins of the
  real prompts. Portuguese and French were added below the 5% traffic rule,
  by decision.
- The `languages` block of the page is internal. Do not pin this page in the
  frontend.
