## Headline

- **The agent stops before fetching data.** On 49 failures it chose the place or the dataset on most attempts and then never pulled data. On 26 of them it asked the user to pick a dataset (a nudge), for example "Is palm-related deforestation risk higher in Sabah or Sarawak?" and "Which of Maranhão, Tocantins and Piauí had the most natural vegetation conversion since 2021?" This is still the largest single lever, as on 29 September and 1 October.
- **Causal questions skip the data.** On 16 failures the agent answered from `search_blogs` without pulling data, 7 of them causal ("Why are our forests in Riau being burned?", "Why is deforestation increasing in Pará?"). Causal is the weakest type for the third run running.
- **Real questions hold up; synthetic ones do not.** Real CHALLENGE questions pass at 83%, synthetic ones at 52%. Part of that gap is the agent (above) and part is the drafted expectations (below).
- **Picker misses on named areas:** Western North and Ashanti resolved to Ghana as a whole; Odzala-Kokoua dropped out of a two-park comparison; the Wampis territory, Offinso and "our concessions in Madre de Dios and Loreto" did not resolve at all.
- **Open-ended windows end in 2025.** For "since 2021" and similar the agent pulled 2021 to 2025 on every attempt, which confirms the decision to expect 2025-12-31 where the sheet wrote 2024-12-31 (about 8 questions would otherwise fail their date check).

## Recommendations

**Upstream (project-zeno):**

1. **Pull after choosing.** When the question names the place, the dataset and the period, the agent should fetch the data rather than offer a dataset choice. 26 failures.
2. **Route "why" questions to the driver and fire data** before blog search (16 failures, 7 causal). Unchanged from 1 October.
3. **Multi-area picks:** keep each named area in a comparison (Western North and Ashanti, Odzala-Kokoua and Lopé). Feed the unresolved names (Wampis, Offinso) into PZB-1392.

**Case set (before the November freeze):**

4. **Fix 9 expectation errors.** On these the expected answer is wrong and fixing it flips the verdict (North Star 63.2% to about 66.5%):
   - The **portfolio "redirect" expectation is out of date.** The agent pulled real data for all 144 municipalities of Pará (`ch-port-018`), all nine Bolivian departments (`ch-quant-189`) and African countries (`ch-comp-168`; its ranking still needs checking). Many-area analysis exists. Engineering should confirm what is supported and the sheet's "Not yet: expect clear redirect" rows should be re-classified.
   - **Either/or rows that also expect a dataset.** `ch-mon-055` and `ch-comp-166` passed their judged text with an honest "not available", but they also expect dataset 11 or 4, which a decline never selects. Drop the dataset and date expectations on either/or rows.
   - **Emissions from loss:** the tree cover loss dataset reports emissions from loss, so `ch-quant-169` (Colombia 2023) and `ch-trend-078` (Brazil 2020–2024) should accept dataset 4 as well as 6.
   - **Defensible alternative areas:** Salonga as the World Heritage site (WDPA 10906; `ch-trend-079`) and the Kakataibo landscape as the Kakataibo Sur reserve (`ch-quant-182`). These need an any-of AOI check or a reviewed choice.

   Five more have a wrong expectation but also fail on agent behaviour on most attempts, so a fix alone does not flip them: `ch-port-017` (asks back, then ranks Côte d'Ivoire only and ignores the cocoa filter), `ch-mon-058` (asks back although the alerts' natural-lands layer answers it), `ch-quant-168` (the Salonga id, plus it asks back), `ch-mon-056` (Mahakam Delta: one attempt used Kutai Kartanegara, two could not find it) and `ch-comp-176` (accept natural lands (3), but two attempts asked back). `ch-mon-047` (printable map of the Aru Islands) got a report with a map; decide whether that counts.
5. **Review the other drafted expectations**, especially the judged `text` checks on method questions (`method` cohorts), which several careful answers failed.
6. **Seed a test account** (Test Area A and the 25-polygon Test Portfolio) so the 30 excluded rows can run, or drop them from the version.

**Harness:** none blocking. Run-relative windows (`{run-Nd}`, `{month_start}`, `{last_month_start}`, `{current_year}-01-01`) behaved as intended, and the run had no errors.
