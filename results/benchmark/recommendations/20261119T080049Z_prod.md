# BENCHMARK 2026-languages: first full run, Spanish and Indonesian, 19 November 2026

**Combined run of the languages companion `2026-languages` (draft), before the
production update of 19 November.** This is the "before" measurement; the same
runs repeat after the update. The language rates sit beside the English North
Star and never count in it.

Three runs on one day, production, default profile, 3 trials:

| Run | What it measured |
|---|---|
| `20261119T063036Z_prod` | The 61 English twins that are members of `2026` (a partial, non-canonical run of `2026`; used only as the English side of the pairs) |
| `20261119T072356Z_prod` | The 61 Spanish and Indonesian questions that pair with those twins |
| `20261119T080049Z_prod` | The other 198 companion members: 99 language questions and their 99 English twins (10 workers; no timeout) |

The page `pages/20261119T080049Z_prod.json` combines them
(`benchmark_page.py --base`, twice). All 160 pairs are measured; none is
unpaired or unscored.

- **Reply language** (info-only): 236 of 236 scored Spanish replies and 237 of

- **Failure modes of the language questions** (majority verdict): Spanish

- **The 61 `2026` twins against the 27/28 October run** (`20261028T124051Z_prod`):
  47 of 61 right today, 48 then; 3 newly wrong (`ch-quant-134`, `ch-comp-043`,
  `ch-quant-107`), 2 newly right (`ch-quant-170`, `ch-quant-189`). Replies that
  ask the user back rose from 16 to 27 of 183.

## How to read it

- The Spanish gap leans one way (8 pairs against 2) and its interval ends
  close to zero. The Indonesian differences run both ways and are within
  normal variation.
- The real user prompts are harder than the translations on both sides of a
  pair. Their expectations were checked without an agent run and adjusted
  after a 40-pair, 1-trial pilot (18 November), so some failures can still be
  case problems, on both sides alike.
- 30 pairs differ between the two sides. Nobody has read them for their cause
  (agent, judge or case) yet.

## Caveats

- **Scored before two fixes to the number reader.** These runs used the
  harness at 8b8ee76 and 7a91cab. Fixed since: Spanish and Indonesian zero
  phrases that matched sentences stating no zero (cc78ebb, on main). Not yet
  on main: a number grouped with spaces ("1 000 ha") could match an expected
  figure by a fragment. Both can only turn a wrong answer into a right one.
  On 2,294 Spanish and Indonesian replies from the traces the fixes changed
  four; the results of these runs were not rescored.
- **Two errata on `2026`** (`ch-comp-090`, `ch-trend-052`) were added with the
  language cases. Their corrected copies `ch-comp-185` and `ch-trend-098` are
  the twins of `ch-comp-090-id` and `ch-trend-052-id`. Follow-up: PZB-1721.
- No native speaker has reviewed the translations or the English twins of the
  real prompts.
- The `languages` block of the page is internal until the frontend parses it.
  Do not pin this page in the frontend.
