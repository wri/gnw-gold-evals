## 1. For the agent team

I read the attempts of the 10 questions that passed on 19 November and fail
now, and compared them with the 19 November attempts.

**#927 fixed what it set out to fix.** All three questions it targeted now
pass: `ch-mon-022` (which DRC province, 0 of 3 to 3 of 3), `ch-port-015` and
`ch-quant-174` (Peru's Indigenous territories). Four more gains probably
follow from the same change, because the area selected changed: `ch-port-003` (every Brazilian state, now 3 of 3),
`ch-causal-026` (Côte d'Ivoire's cocoa belt resolves to its regions),
`ch-mon-035` (the Wampis territory is found) and `ch-mon-054` (the logging
concessions around Lomié).

**#927 broke one question, and probably two more.**

- `ch-causal-013`, "Is logging responsible for forest loss in Gabon?", 3 of 3
  to 0 of 3. The words around the place now trigger subregion selection:
  "logging" selects Gabon's logging concessions, there are more than 50, and
  the agent declines on every attempt. On 19 November it pulled the drivers
  data for Gabon and answered. I am sure of this one: the reply names the
  concessions limit on all three attempts.
- `ch-aoi-118`, "Show me the Cerrado", 2 of 3 to 0 of 3. The agent now zooms
  to a small place named Cerrado (Cerrado de Riberalta in Bolivia twice, a
  place in Britânia once) and does not ask whether the biome was meant. Likely
  #927's area prompt changes; not confirmed.
- `ch-aoi-128`, "Go to 9.5N, 79.5W", 3 of 3 to 1 of 3. The agent now places
  the point in Panama, and once says "I've centered the map on those
  coordinates", which it cannot do. Likely the same cause; not confirmed.

**One question may now be stricter than the answer deserves.**
`ch-trend-086` ("Where should we focus fire prevention in Santa Cruz?") failed
one attempt that ranked Santa Cruz's provinces, a #927 effect and arguably the
better answer, because the case expects the department. The owner decides if
the case should accept it (an erratum on a frozen member).

**The other five look like run-to-run variation, not an agent change.**
`ch-aoi-155` and `ch-quant-166`: replies like the ones that passed on
19 November, failed by the AI reviewer this time. `ch-quant-163`: the agent
skipped the pull on 2 of 3 attempts (1 of 3 before). `ch-comp-076`: on 2 of 3
attempts the reply states 133,271 ha where its own pull gives 100,331 ha (1 of
3 before). `ch-quant-189`: see section 3.
