## Recommendations

**Upstream (project-zeno):** unchanged from 23 October. "Pull after
choosing" would flip at least `ch-port-003`, `ch-quant-168` and `ch-mon-058`.
The natural-lands question (`ch-mon-058`) is a clean test of it: the alerts'
`natural_lands` layer answers it directly. "Mahakam Delta" not matching the
KBA "Delta Mahakam" belongs with PZB-1392.

**Case set (before the November freeze):**

1. **`ch-quant-194` (canopy height): reword so the judge cannot require the
   optional part.** The text says the answer "may point to" Land & Carbon
   Lab or GFW; the judge read that as required and failed two honest
   answers. Proposed: "Says that Horizon has no canopy height data and does
   not invent a canopy height layer." AJ decided pointing elsewhere is
   optional, so the text should not mention it.
2. **`ch-port-011` (rank the worst 10 World Heritage forest sites): decide
   what counts.** The 24 October text asks the agent to say there are too
   many sites. The real limit is different: `pick_aoi` cannot select World
   Heritage sites as a group. One attempt said exactly that and offered
   alternatives; two answered from `search_blogs` with a cited top 10 from
   UNESCO/WRI/IUCN research. The old text passed 2 of 3. Options: accept an
   honest "cannot select these sites as a group" plus alternatives, and
   either accept or reject a cited ranking from published research.
3. Both fixes mint new uids, so they need a 2-case rerun (6 attempts).
   `benchmark_page.py --base` takes one base today; combining a third run
   needs `--base` to accept a chain (small change), or a fresh rerun of all
   revised members.
4. **Still open from 23 October:** correct the BEF sheet's `capability`
   facet on the reclassified many-area rows (it still says "Not yet: expect
   clear redirect" and is copied into the cases on every rebuild), review
   the other drafted expectations (113 untouched since 23 October), and seed
   or drop the 30 fixture rows.

**Harness:** none blocking. The any-of AOI check, `--missing-from` and the
combined page behaved as intended, and the run had no errors.
